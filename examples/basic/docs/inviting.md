# Inviting teammates

Open **Members**, type the person's email under **Invite a teammate** and press **Send invite**. They get an email with a link to join; the link works for 7 days.

## Who can be invited

Only people whose email address is in one of the workspace's **allowed email domains**. An invite to any other address is refused with "Invites to @domain are not allowed in this workspace."

To invite someone from another domain (a contractor on gmail.com, say), add their domain under **Allowed email domains** first and press **Save domains**, then send the invite again. Remove the domain afterwards if you only needed it once: people who already joined stay.
